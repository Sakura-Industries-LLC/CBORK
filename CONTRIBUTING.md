# Bug Reports & Contributions

This repository is maintained as a public, read-only distribution.
We do not host a public issue tracker or accept unsolicited public pull requests.

If you have found a bug or wish to propose a modification, you may do so via your own public fork:

1. **Fork this repository** to your own account.
2. **Create an isolated test case** that explicitly demonstrates the failure or feature.
3. **Commit your proposed fix** so that the test case/feature passes.
4. **Email a link to your fork** (including the specific commit hashes for the test and the fix) to
   `submissions@sakuraindustries.net`.

Sending a fork link is a proposal only.
It does not mean the work has been reviewed, accepted, or incorporated into this project.
If we decide to consider incorporating your work, we may contact you with next steps,
including any contributor terms that must be agreed before acceptance.

**Please Note:** This is not a core product for our company.
We are releasing it in the hope it proves useful, with no guarantees of further work.
As we have very little engineering resources we can dedicate to this project,
no proposal in this form should expect a timely response, or even a review.
We genuinely appreciate useful proposals, but do not have the resources to effectively vet them if demand is high,
or if the proposed changes are large.

If your proposal aligns with our current roadmap and meets our architectural standards, we may contact you by email with next steps.

This project is still evolving.
Public availability does not imply API stability, CLI stability, output-format stability,
or semantic version/compatibility guarantees.
We may change behavior, restructure internals, rewrite diagnostics, remove features, or break downstream forks without notice.

You are welcome to fork the project and adapt it for your own use case.
Forks are independent projects, and we do not guarantee compatibility with them.

CBORK is not a registered trademark, but we ask downstream maintainers to rename durable forks to avoid confusion with this project.
